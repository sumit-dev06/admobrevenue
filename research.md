## 1. Google AdSense Revenue Mechanics

### 1.1 The Revenue Share Model
* **AdSense for Content**: Google passes **68%** of the advertiser's bid to the publisher. For every $1.00 paid by an advertiser on Google Ads, the publisher receives $0.68.
* **AdSense for Search**: The revenue share is **51%** for publisher search queries.
* **Shift to Impression-Based Payment**: In recent updates, Google transitioned to paying publishers predominantly by impressions (CPM basis) alongside traditional click-based (CPC) bids, aligning AdSense with programmatic header bidding standards.

### 1.3 AdSense Format Performance Multipliers
- **Mobile Sticky Anchor (Overlay)**: 1.45x multiplier (92%–96% Viewability).
- **Vignette Interstitials (Page Transitions)**: 2.20x multiplier (96% Viewability, high CPM).
- **Sticky Sidebar (300x600)**: 1.35x multiplier on Desktop viewports.
- **In-Article Native Units**: 1.15x multiplier for first 3 units, diminishing returns thereafter.
- **Top Header Billboard (728x90 / 970x250)**: 1.25x multiplier (Prime above-the-fold visibility).
- **Multiplex Related Content Grid**: 0.90x multiplier (End of content recommendation).

### 2.3 Operating System Multipliers
* **iOS (Apple App Store)**: Users demonstrate significantly higher in-app purchase conversion rates and advertiser intent. Tier 1 iOS traffic yields a **+25% to +45% eCPM premium** over Android.
* **Android (Google Play)**: Higher global volume and fill rates in Tier 2 and Tier 3 markets, with lower average CPMs in Tier 1.

---

### 2.4 Mediation & Open Bidding Impact
Integrating **AdMob Mediation** with real-time bidding partners (AppLovin MAX, Unity Ads, Mintegral, Meta Audience Network, ironSource) replaces waterfall latency with simultaneous programmatic auctions:
* **Blended eCPM Lift**: **+15% to +35%**
* **Fill Rate Improvement**: **+5% to +10%**
